# Contract: the licence statement

What a specification document shows its reader (FR-003), and what the site publishes as part of the document's cover.

## Form

The last row of the header table, the table directly under the document's title and version line:

```markdown
| Licence | [Apache License 2.0](https://github.com/etalii-adp/etalii.adp/blob/develop/LICENSE) (`Apache-2.0`) |
```

Rendered: **Licence** — [Apache License 2.0](https://github.com/etalii-adp/etalii.adp/blob/develop/LICENSE) (`Apache-2.0`).

The cells are padded to the table's column widths where the table is aligned (DISL, DID, FBL) and left unpadded where it is not (the placeholders).

## What does not change

No version, no `Date` row, no schema, no example (research R3).

## A new specification

Copies the row unchanged. Nothing else is registered anywhere: the check finds the document by its name (FR-005).
