# Inventory: persistence of the diagram definitions

What each of the 25 definitions in `definitions/diagrams/` needs from persistence, read from its `.dis` (`persistence`, `plugins`, `x-adp` keys) and its companion `.md` notes on `develop` at `e908a19`, and what it becomes under FBL (FR-091). "Declared" means an FBL binding with the `declared` reader; "plugin reader" means an FBL binding whose reader is a persistence plugin under the contract; rewriting the definitions is follow-up work.

The registration layout block ("RL block" below) is `layout:` followed by `  <id>: <x> <y>` lines, only for dragged elements, sorted by id, `0.###` numbers, CRLF, removed when empty, everything above it preserved byte for byte (`helm-chart.md` "Persistence", `dotnet-dependency-graph.md` "The binding", `ansible-structure.md` §6).

Totals (SC-003): of the 24 plugin-backed definitions, 15 become declared bindings (C4 ×6, databricks ×3, causal-loop, dependency graph, functional decomposition, hype cycle, mindmap, Wardley), 1 becomes a declared binding with a plugin reader for part of its reading (Azure), and 8 keep a plugin reader under the contract (W3C ×4, SPARQL, .NET, Ansible, Helm). The timeline moves from DISL's `yaml` format to a declared binding.

Routing and registration keys found, all of which have an FBL equivalent (SC-005): `x-adp` `claimsBareFiles`, `sharedExtension` / `x-adp-shared-extension`, `suggestWhenBodyContains`, `documentExtensions`, `subject: folder`, `registrationHeaders`, `family`, `extensions`; `x-adp-origin`, `x-adp-state`; `persistence.x-ghg-header`; registration headers `body`, `view`, `resource`, `language`, `layout`.

Gaps the notes attribute to plugins that FBL answers: a plugin cannot report problems (`dotnet-dependency-graph.md`); text findings belong to the plugin and have no rule (`causal-loop-diagram.md`); there is no way to declare new-document bytes (`w3c-skos.md` §2, `wardley-map.md` §2).
