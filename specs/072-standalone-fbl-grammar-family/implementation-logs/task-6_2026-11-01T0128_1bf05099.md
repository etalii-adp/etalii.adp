# Implementation Log: Task 6

**Summary:** A body of the grammar family is written as FBL 0.9 sections 4.9 and 6.3 say (standalone PR #787, merge `1ccfa40`; tasks taken from etalii.adp `4696ce0`).

What the family writes:

- **Set.** One `replace-value` of the value's span, as text, through a `map`. Numbers and booleans are written as FBL 6.3 writes them. A set of an attribute whose node is absent is refused. An emptied value is set, never removed.
- **Add.** One `insert-entry` of the rule's `emit`.
  - Each line feed in `emit` is written as the body's line ending plus the entry's indentation.
  - The entry is placed `after-last`, `end`, `next-sibling`, `start` or `end-of-document` in the container `insert.container` selects, and the body keeps or lacks its final line ending.
  - An add after an entry goes right after that entry, as in the `lines` family. `EditPlanner` lets it through for the family.
- **Removal.** One `remove-entry` of the entry's line span, with its leading comments, or of its own span.
- **Read-back check.** Before a set or an add is planned, the body is read again with the edit applied. An edit that would not read back, or that would leave more text skipped, is refused, and nothing is written.

`FamilyReader.Options` is now protected (commit `877a3a0f1`), so the family reads again with its own options. Without that, a CS9107 capture of the options would have stood.

`w3c-turtle-grammar` and `w3c-turtle-grammar-partial` leave `Pending`, and both pass byte for byte with their splices and undos. A planted offset makes the runner fail.

`Grammar/GrammarWriting.Tests.cs` covers:

- the set, with numbers and booleans;
- every placement, and the add after an entry;
- a body without a final newline, and CRLF with a multi-line `emit`;
- removal with a leading comment;
- both read-back refusals;
- a rename that rewrites references;
- undo and redo refused on drift.

Each test was seen to fail under sabotage.

The commits were `f0583289a` (the writing), `877a3a0f1` (the options) and `14c605ea8`. In `14c605ea8`, the test bodies are UTF-8 literals, after inspect.sh's `UseUtf8StringLiteral` finding.

The branch was merged with `origin/develop` after #786. The four gates exited zero (18,428 tests, 0 failed), and inspect.sh is clean.
