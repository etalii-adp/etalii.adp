# Interpretation, Fidelity and Notion Styling Checklist: The Gartner Hype Cycle Graph as a Notion Add-on

**Purpose**: Test the quality of the requirements for interpreting the DISL specification and the FBL binding, drawing a document as the other hosts do, the toolbox and the property grid and their reuse, looking like Notion, and being usable with a keyboard and assistive technology: are they complete, clear, consistent and measurable enough to write tasks from?
**Created**: 2026-10-08
**Feature**: [notion-hype-cycle-addon.spec.md](../notion-hype-cycle-addon.spec.md), with [plan.md](../plan.md), [research.md](../research.md), [contracts/shared-parts.md](../contracts/shared-parts.md), [contracts/addon-address.md](../contracts/addon-address.md) and [contracts/published-tree.md](../contracts/published-tree.md)

**Note**: This custom checklist is generated by the `/speckit-checklist` command based on feature context and requirements.
**Review Ownership**: This checklist is a reviewer-owned requirements-quality review artifact. Mark an item `[x]` only when the reviewer determines the requirements-quality criterion is satisfied.
**Marker Semantics**: `[x]` means the criterion has been reviewed and satisfied for requirements quality. It does not mean implementation work is complete.

**Depth**: standard. **Audience**: the reviewer of the `etalii.adp` pull request, before the tasks step. **Focus**: the interpreter and its generality, fidelity of the drawing, the panels and their reuse, Notion styling, keyboard and assistive technology, the examples and the truthfulness requirements. The store, undo, access, the service and secrets are in [storage.md](storage.md) and are not repeated here.
