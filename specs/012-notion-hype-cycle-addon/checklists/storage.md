# Storage, Undo and Access Checklist: The Gartner Hype Cycle Graph as a Notion Add-on

**Purpose**: Test the quality of the requirements for keeping a graph in a Notion database, undoing and redoing edits there, and reaching the database through the service: are they complete, clear, consistent and measurable enough to write tasks from?
**Created**: 2026-10-08
**Feature**: [notion-hype-cycle-addon.spec.md](../notion-hype-cycle-addon.spec.md), with [plan.md](../plan.md), [research.md](../research.md), [contracts/store.md](../contracts/store.md) and [contracts/service.md](../contracts/service.md)

**Note**: This custom checklist is generated by the `/speckit-checklist` command based on feature context and requirements.
**Review Ownership**: This checklist is a reviewer-owned requirements-quality review artifact. Mark an item `[x]` only when the reviewer determines the requirements-quality criterion is satisfied.
**Marker Semantics**: `[x]` means the criterion has been reviewed and satisfied for requirements quality. It does not mean implementation work is complete.

**Depth**: standard. **Audience**: the reviewer of the `etalii.adp` pull request, before the tasks step. **Focus**: the store and its integrity under edits, undo and concurrent change; access, the service and secrets. Fidelity of the drawing, the interpreter's generality, Notion styling and accessibility are left to a checklist of their own.
